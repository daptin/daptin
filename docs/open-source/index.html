01

Own the data plane

Daptin persists application state in SQLite, MySQL, MariaDB, or PostgreSQL and connects files to local or configured cloud storage. There is no mandatory hosted control plane between your clients and your backend.

02

Own the deployment

Run a downloadable binary, build from source, package it in a container, or schedule it on your preferred infrastructure. Enable only the protocols your application needs.

03

Own the contract

Your schema is YAML or JSON, your interfaces are standard HTTP, JSON:API, GraphQL, OpenAPI, WebSocket, OAuth/OIDC, and OpenAI-compatible routes, and your behavior is inspectable in source.

AN HONEST TRADE

Control replaces platform dependency—not operational responsibility.

Self-hosting removes a required vendor runtime, but production reliability still belongs to the operator. Daptin provides the application controls; your deployment provides the service discipline.

What Daptin gives you

  • Integrated backend capabilities in one runtime
  • Standard database and protocol choices
  • Source-visible behavior and upgrade history
  • No required cloud account or per-seat platform plan
  • Configurable services and portable schemas
  • Health, statistics, audit, cache, TLS, and graceful shutdown controls

What production still requires

  • Database backups and tested restores
  • Stable JWT and encryption secrets
  • HTTPS, DNS, and certificate operations
  • Durable storage and capacity planning
  • Monitoring, alerting, and log retention
  • Upgrade, security, and incident processes
GROW WITHOUT CHANGING THE MODEL

A practical deployment path.

  1. STEP 1

    Evaluate

    One binary, SQLite, and local asset storage on a developer machine.

  2. STEP 2

    Launch

    PostgreSQL or MySQL, persistent storage, HTTPS, backups, and monitoring.

  3. STEP 3

    Coordinate

    Multiple instances with shared database and Olric-backed distributed runtime behavior.
